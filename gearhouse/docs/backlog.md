# Backlog

This list holds work moved to a later stage than the current one, so it does not sit in the plan as a promise.

- Starting as a repair shop: the repair-job loop of car in, diagnosis, parts, labour, then the bill. This is stage 3.
- Starting as a large firm: the "decide only" screens for board agenda, department reports and budgets. This is stage 4.
- Competitor AI, the economic model beyond the 1930s slump, and research tree part 1.
- Family, will, death and succession. The vertical slice keeps only the hidden lifespan.
- Radio, motorsport, and crashes beyond panel damage.
- Countries other than the USA.
- The game's end year, the split into period parts, and the target play time for part 1. These are decided before stage 5.
- Calendar during real-time scenes. The proposal is to pause the calendar during the scene and add the time it used when it ends.
- Name data: the starter lists hold about 58,000 surname and given-name pairs (about 1.2 million with middle initials). Replace them with about 1,000 surnames and 1,000 given names per country from census and Social Security statistics, with weights by birth year.
- People still to build: applicants and hiring routes, interviews and trials, satisfaction, pay demands, leaving, traits acting on work, portraits.
- Money still to build: banks and loans, bank failures, the per-car profit sheet, the yearly tax, bankruptcy.
- Supply still to build: finding suppliers through the directory and adverts (with the newspaper), inspection of deliveries, relations acting on credit and priority, price changes and closures from the economy, long contracts, incidents (bad batch, price rise, offers), the founder's time for visits.
- Build still to build: the three-layer body edit feeding the design effort, the calculated performance figures on a finished design, test drives finding defects, fixing defects, rushing, tools, the hands-on panel step, small incidents during a build.
- Balance to check with the five-year run: a founder's weak skills (a coachbuilder's 28 in mechanics, 31 in welding) give step qualities of 38-47 and about three defects on the first car.
