Notes, to verify per piece:
- **USA:** compositions published in 1930 or earlier are in the public domain from 2026, after a 95-year term. Sound recordings from 1923–1946 are protected for 100 years.
- **Korea and EU:** compositions are protected for the composer's life plus 70 years.
- Period recordings are therefore not used. Pieces are re-arranged and re-recorded, or written new in the period style.
